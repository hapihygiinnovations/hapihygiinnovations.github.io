Biotech Engineer

Job Description:

As a Biotech Engineer, you will work on designing and testing innovative materials and disinfection solutions at the intersection of chemistry, materials science, and biotechnology. This role offers the opportunity to engage in research that is both academically challenging and commercially relevant, while working closely with IIT Bombay alumni and IIT Gandhinagar professors.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct research on advanced materials with water- and stain-resistant, antibacterial properties
  • Characterize disinfection technologies and evaluate their efficacy
  • Perform laboratory experiments in material modification, coatings, and disinfection testing
  • Collaborate with the team to translate lab findings into scalable product applications

What We Offer

  • Internship:
    • Stipend: ₹5,000/month + upto ₹5,000 performance bonus
    • PPO opportunities for exceptional candidates with a CTC range of ₹3–6 LPA
  • Full-time:
    • CTC: ₹3–6 LPA (Based on expertise and role fit)
  • Certificate & Letter of Recommendation
  • Flat, ownership-driven culture with direct mentorship from founders and professors
  • Long-term opportunity to contribute to IP creation and product innovation

What You"ll Gain

  • Hands-on research experience in advanced antibacterial and water-resistant materials
  • Exposure to startup culture and the process of translating lab research into real-world applications
  • Direct mentorship from IIT Bombay alumni and IIT Gandhinagar professors
  • Opportunity to publish, patent, and contribute to technologies with global potential

Desired Candidate Profile

  • Bachelor’s/Master’s in Materials Science, Biotechnology, or related fields
  • Hands-on experience in material processing, modification, or chemical experiments
  • Familiarity with laboratory practices, characterization techniques, and safety protocols
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail
  • Zeal to work in a fast-paced, innovation-driven startup environment

Hiring Process

  • Application Screening: Resume + portfolio
  • Design Assignment: A task to access your technical and creative ability
  • Interview (Google Meet or In-person): Discussion on assignment, problem-solving approach, and fit with the team

How to Apply

  • Please share your resume with your portfolio to core.atsc@gmail.com
