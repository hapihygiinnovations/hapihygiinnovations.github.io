Business & Strategy Analyst

Job Description:

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys working across finance, strategy, and market research. You will work directly with the founders to shape business plans, prepare key documents, and support fundraising and go-to-market efforts. It is a high-ownership position designed for someone who wants broad exposure across multiple business functions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare financial statements including projected P&L, cash flow statements and balance sheets
  • Conduct detailed market analysis, competitor benchmarking and customer-segment studies
  • Design go-to-market strategies for different products and use cases
  • Create customised pitch decks for customers, partners, and investors
  • Support application writing for government grants, accelerator programs and investor pitches
  • Assist founders in preparing datasheets, business proposals and internal reports
  • Work closely with product and engineering teams to align business strategy with development roadmap

What We Offer

  • Internship:
    • Stipend: ₹5,000/month + upto ₹5,000 performance bonus
    • PPO opportunities for exceptional candidates with a CTC range of ₹3–6 LPA
  • Full-time:
    • CTC: ₹3–6 LPA (Based on expertise and role fit)
  • Certificate & Letter of Recommendation
  • Flat, ownership-driven culture with direct mentorship from founders and mentors
  • Exposure to real-world scenarios across fundraising, partnerships, and GTM planning

What You"ll Gain

  • Hands-on experience in early-stage startup building
  • Strong understanding of financial modelling, customer segmentation, and market strategy
  • Experience in grant applications, accelerator selections and investor readiness
  • Chance to significantly influence product positioning and business expansion

Desired Candidate Profile

  • Degree in business, finance, economics, or equivalent experience
  • Strong skills in financial modelling and presentation building
  • Excellent market research, analysis, and writing skills
  • Ability to prepare pitch decks and articulate strategic narratives
  • Comfortable working in a fast-paced startup environment

Hiring Process

  • Application Screening: Resume + portfolio
  • Design Assignment: A task to access your technical and creative ability
  • Interview (Google Meet or In-person): Discussion on assignment, problem-solving approach, and fit with the team

How to Apply
