Electrical Engineer

Job Description:

As an Electrical Engineer, you will play a critical role in designing and integrating electronic systems into our product. You will be responsible for developing and testing circuits, programming microcontrollers, designing PCBs, and building reliable battery management solutions. This role requires strong technical expertise, problem-solving skills, and the ability to work closely with mechanical and product design teams.

Key Responsibilities

  • Circuit design, development, and testing for automation systems
  • Programming microcontrollers (Arduino/ESP32) to control motors, sensors, and power boards
  • PCB design, development, and soldering for prototypes and production
  • Design and testing of battery management and recharging boards with protection circuits
  • Collaborate with mechanical and design teams for seamless system integration

What We Offer

  • Internship:
    • Stipend: ₹5,000/month + upto ₹5,000 performance bonus
    • PPO opportunities for exceptional candidates with a CTC range of ₹3–6 LPA
  • Full-time:
    • CTC: ₹3–6 LPA (Based on expertise and role fit)
  • Certificate & Letter of Recommendation
  • Flat, ownership-driven culture where you work directly with founders
  • Long-term opportunity to build and scale IP-driven products

What You"ll Gain

  • Hands-on experience in embedded systems, PCB design, and battery management
  • Exposure to startup culture and product development from prototype to market launch
  • Opportunity to solve challenging real-world engineering problems with innovative solutions
  • Direct interaction with IIT Bombay alumni and access to startup ecosystem networks

Desired Candidate Profile

  • Bachelor’s/Master’s in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Embedded Systems, or related fields
  • Strong skills in circuit design, Arduino/ESP32 programming, and PCB design
  • Experience with sensors, motors, and power electronics
  • Familiarity with battery management systems and safety protocols
  • Initiative, attention to detail, and passion for building impactful products in a startup environment

Hiring Process

  • Application Screening: Resume + portfolio
  • Design Assignment: A task to access your technical and creative ability
  • Interview (Google Meet or In-person): Discussion on assignment, problem-solving approach, and fit with the team
